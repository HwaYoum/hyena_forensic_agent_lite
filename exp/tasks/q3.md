Article 7 of Hangbok Clothing’s Company Credit Card Management Regulations specifies the cases in which the company credit card may not be used.
- Article 7 (Restrictions on Use) ① To ensure transparency in the use of corporate cards, in the cases falling under each of the following items, the corporate card cannot be used. However, in the cases of items 1 to 3, this does not apply where it is unavoidable for carrying out business and objective proof (business trip, prior internal approval, etc.) is possible.
1. Use on public holidays and days off
2. Use at a distant place significantly outside the jurisdiction and near home
3. Use at abnormal times (late night: after 23:00)
4. Use of securities such as gift certificates for private purposes
② The business types in which use of the corporate card is prohibited are as follows.
1. Entertainment businesses: room salons, entertainment bars, danran bars, nightclubs, etc.
2. Hygiene businesses: hair and beauty salons, skin care salons, saunas, massage parlors, foot massage and other personal services
3. Leisure businesses: indoor and outdoor golf courses, karaoke rooms, social dance, phone rooms, video rooms, etc.
4. Gambling businesses: casinos, lottery shops, arcades, etc.
5. Other businesses: bars, cocktail bars, etc.
6. Other business types that the president recognizes as unsuitable for corporate card use
